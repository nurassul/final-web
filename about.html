Our Identity & Principles

About FORMA Studio

Founded with a conviction that good architecture is quiet, purposeful, and harmoniously connected to nature.

Our Journey

A Decade of Architectural Clarity

Started in 2014, FORMA Studio emerged from a desire to strip away unnecessary ornamentation in modern construction. We believe every wall, column, and window must serve an authentic function.

Our team brings together architects, interior creators, structural engineers, and visual designers under one roof. We supervise each project from initial sketch to final physical realization.

Guiding Standards

Our Fundamental Values

01

Simplicity & Purity

We focus solely on structural proportions, clean lighting angles, and tactile textures without superfluous decorative distractions.

02

Honest Materials

Natural stone, untreated larch, brushed steel, and exposed glass. We let materials express their raw, organic qualities.

03

Human Centricity

Spaces must enhance psychological calm, social connection, and effortless daily movement for those who live and work inside.

Alikhan Nurpeisov — Principal Architect

Alikhan holds a Master of Architecture from Politecnico di Milano. He founded FORMA Studio in 2014 and has overseen over 90 built projects ranging from private mountain villas to public cultural pavilions.

  • Focus: Sustainable civic & residential architecture
  • Key Projects: North Ridge Residence, Kora Cultural Center
  • Recognition: 2023 Central Asian Architecture Laureate
Contact Alikhan

Elena Kim — Head of Interior Design

Elena specializes in bespoke interior landscapes. Her design philosophy revolves around hidden storage systems, continuous floor planes, and diffused natural lighting.

  • Focus: Corporate workspaces, high-end private interiors
  • Key Projects: Prism Office Hub, Glass Pavilion Gallery
  • Experience: 11 years in interior spatial research
Contact Elena

Daniyar Sadykov — Senior Structural Engineer

Daniyar brings strict scientific rigor and computational structural optimization to every blueprint, enabling expansive cantilever designs and slender load-bearing columns.

  • Focus: Seismic engineering, timber hybrid structures
  • Key Projects: Echo Thermal Resort, Monolith Archive
  • Education: PhD in Structural Mechanics
Contact Daniyar

Sarah Adams — Digital Design Director
